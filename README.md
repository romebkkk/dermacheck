## 🌟 Why this matters
Cutaneous malignant melanoma accounts for only ~1% of all skin cancers, but is responsible for the vast majority of skin cancer deaths. When excised in its earliest stages (melanoma in situ or thin Breslow thickness <0.8 mm), the **5-year survival rate exceeds 98%**. Conversely, metastatic melanoma carries a grim prognosis.

Most citizens lack objective tools to recognize suspicious evolving lesions. **DermaCheck** empowers individuals and caregivers to perform structured, evidence-based skin self-exams in under 2 minutes, physically calibrating the clinical 6 mm diameter threshold against an everyday credit card or Spanish DNI on their mobile screen.

## ⚡ Key Modules

1. **📋 ABCDE Rule Evaluation (AEDV / AAD):**
   - **A (Asymmetry):** Mismatched halves.
   - **B (Border):** Ragged, scalloped, or blurred contours.
   - **C (Color):** Multi-shaded pigmentation (black, dark brown, red, white, blue).
   - **D (Diameter):** Lesions >6 mm.
   - **E (Evolution):** Documented changes in size, shape, elevation, bleeding, or pruritus.

2. **🔬 Glasgow 7-Point Checklist (NICE Guidelines):**
   - Evaluates 3 major criteria (change in size, irregular shape, irregular color - 2 pts each) and 4 minor criteria (diameter $\ge 7$ mm, inflammation, crusting/bleeding, sensory change - 1 pt each).
   - Validated threshold: $\ge 3$ points mandates rapid dermatological referral.

3. **📏 Physical Screen Calibration (ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1):**
   - Calibrates the device's pixels-per-millimeter using a physical 85.6 mm card, rendering a 1:1 true-scale 6 mm inspection circle.

4. **🦆 "Ugly Duckling" Sign (Signe du Vilain Petit Canard):**
   - Flags lesions that sharply diverge from the patient's dominant nevus pattern, which carry a 6x higher risk of malignancy.

## 🇪🇸 Español

### ¿Por qué importa?
El melanoma es el cáncer de piel más letal, pero detectado a tiempo (cuando todavía es plano o superficial) tiene una **curación de casi el 99 %**. El gran problema es que muchas personas esperan meses o años creyendo que es un simple lunar inofensivo.

**DermaCheck** te ayuda a autoexplorarte con rigor médico en 2 minutos: aplica la regla ABCDE y el test de Glasgow, y calibra la pantalla de tu móvil con tu DNI para comprobar si la mancha mide más de 6 mm con precisión milimétrica real.
